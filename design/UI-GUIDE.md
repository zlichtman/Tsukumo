# UX/UI guide: iPhone and Mac

How Tsukumo's screens look and behave, so the iPhone app (`apps/ios`) and the Mac app (`apps/macos`, Tsukumo's Dock) stay consistent. Both are built on TsukumoKit's TsukumoUI, and the Mac's side dock on TsukumoDock. When code and this guide disagree, fix one of them in the same change. The standing rules are in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md); they win over this guide.

Wording (AGENTS.md rule 14): anything a person sees says KemoSabe, or the companion's own name, never "Kemo"; a person's agent is "<Name>'s secure assistant".

Pictures, light and dark: [`onboarding/`](onboarding/) (the first run on both devices), [`bot-customization/`](bot-customization/) (the bot editors and KemoSabe's settings), [`mac-settings/`](mac-settings/) (every Mac Settings page), and [`mac-preview/`](mac-preview/) (the side dock).

## Principles

1. **One control per job.** No duplicated buttons: one Settings window on the Mac, one close button per sheet. On a Mac, Tsukumo is the side dock: nothing chooses where the bots live.
2. **The same app on both devices.** Settings follow one order and one set of names (Account, Bots, Models, KemoSabe), the first run and the bot editors are the same TsukumoUI views, and a device adds only what's its own (the Mac's Dock and General pages).
3. **KemoSabe is always the same.** Its cloud, its name, Apple's on-device model, its job, and its scope never change; its color is the one thing the owner picks. Every other bot can be customized freely.
4. **Say what happened.** KemoSabe's card shows what was shared, what stayed on the device, and what was never read. A build without a capability explains itself in calm secondary text, never as a warning.
5. **Quiet chrome.** Icon buttons are borderless with a soft hover or pressed highlight. The accent marks state (selected, tagged, waiting on you), not decoration.
6. **Personality changes tone only,** never a bot's capability or access.

## The iPhone app

The app in `apps/ios`, on TsukumoUI (`TsukumoKit/Sources/TsukumoUI`), which the Mac app shares. Pictures: `apps/ios/Screenshots/`.

- **The chat is the whole app.** No tab bar. The header: the drawer button (`sidebar.leading`) at the left, Tsukumo's mark and wordmark, and Settings (`gearshape`) at the right, both 42 pt round buttons at the ink's 6.5% fill. Settings and Activity open as sheets with Done.
- **The conversations drawer** slides in from the left (the button, or a swipe from the left 16 pt), up to 330 pt or 86% of the width, over the chat dimmed to 40% black (tap it, or swipe left, to close). From the top: "Chats", New chat (a coral-tinted row), your chats newest first (the bots that spoke, the title, when it last moved; the open chat highlighted; swipe to delete), and Activity at the bottom with its count. Picking a chat closes the drawer and shows it.
- **The drawer's chats** have a menu (touch and hold): Keep on This iPhone (a Device only chat: it leaves iCloud and your Mac, and shows a small iPhone mark) or Sync This Chat, and Delete.
- **The chat looks like the website demo.** Apricot: plum `211B2C` behind cream text in dark, warm paper `FBF6EE` in light, coral `EF705B` for KemoSabe's card and send. 24 pt gutters, 26 pt between rows. The owner's message: a bubble at the ink's 6.5%, 20 pt corners, 15 pt padding, at most 228 pt of text (so a long line wraps as on the website). A bot's reply: its 28 pt avatar, its name (caption, semibold, secondary), and the text (body, 4 pt line spacing). A bot is its clay character with its engine's mark in the corner; KemoSabe is its companion artwork in a circle.
- **KemoSabe's card:** KemoSabe's avatar with a ring and lock in its color, its name, and a card (its color at 7%, 20% stroke, 16 pt corners, 12 pt padding, at most 460 pt): "Claude asked KemoSabe" (caption, semibold, `lock.shield`), the question in quotes, then "Why: …" and "Looking on this iPhone…" with the searching dots while it reads, or the consent prompt (Allow always, Allow once, Don't allow) the first time, or a share card for one Sensitive item (Share, Don't share); then the answer (body, semibold), "Stayed on this iPhone: …" (`lock`), "Not read: …", "Not shared: …", "Shared: “…”", and "On this iPhone · Apple on-device" (`apple.intelligence`). The bot's working row steps aside while its card is live. "Answered Claude: “…”" shows in a capsule at the top for 6 seconds.
- **The stage:** a new chat shows KemoSabe (200 pt), "What’s on your mind?" (34 pt, semibold), "Make room for your day.", and three suggestions. Once you send, KemoSabe (150 pt) stays above the conversation, at its computer while it reads, until the first reply or answer, then steps away.
- **The composer:** the message field ("Message Claude…", naming who it goes to), then + (make a bot), the bots as chips (avatar and name; tagged ones coral), and send (coral circle; stop while bots work); "KemoSabe assists on this iPhone" under them. Typing "@" offers the bots as a strip above.
- **Activity:** a segmented filter (All, KemoSabe, System One, Bots), days newest first, each row with its icon, title, kind, time, and detail.

## The first run, the account, and the bots (both devices)

These are TsukumoUI's views, the same on iPhone and Mac.

- **The first run** (`OnboardingFlow`; pictures in [`onboarding/`](onboarding/)): four steps with progress dots at the top and the buttons at the bottom, on the chat's paper or plum. On the Mac it shows in its own 520 × 720 window before the dock appears.
  1. **Welcome:** KemoSabe's cloud (200 pt), the wordmark, and one line ("All your bots in one chat. KemoSabe keeps what’s personal on this iPhone and shares only the answers you allow."), Get started.
  2. **Sign in:** "Make it yours", why (sync through your own private iCloud; keys and what KemoSabe reads never sync), Sign in with Apple (black capsule in light, white in dark), and Continue without an account (sync stays off; sign in later in Settings).
  3. **Connect what you use**, all optional: Apple Intelligence's status in plain words, Claude and OpenAI with a key field and Add (Connected with a green check once saved; keys stay in this device's Keychain), and what KemoSabe may read (Calendar, Reminders, each with Personal, Sensitive, or Device only); Continue, or Skip for now.
  4. **Your bots:** KemoSabe's card with a lock (always here), the three starters as cards to tap on and off (their character, name, and job), any bot you made, and a dashed Make your own; Start chatting lands in the chat (on the Mac, the dock).

  It shows once; Settings, Account has the account afterwards.
- **Settings, Account** (the first page on both devices): the name, "With Apple, on this iPhone" or "Your bots and chats stay on this iPhone.", sync's line with its icon ("Syncing with iCloud", "Off: no account", "Waiting for iCloud to be turned on for Tsukumo", "Off: sign in to iCloud in Settings", "Sync paused") and its detail, then Sign Out (destructive) or Sign in with Apple (Apple's own button: black in light, white in dark; a 50 pt capsule on iPhone, a Mac control's height of 32 pt with Apple's corners and at most 375 pt wide on the Mac), and what syncs and what never does. A build without the capability explains itself only after a tap, in calm secondary text ("Sign in with Apple isn’t turned on in this build of Tsukumo yet…"), never as an orange warning.
- **Make a bot** (a sheet on iPhone, a sheet from Settings or the panel beside the dock on the Mac; pictures in [`bot-customization/`](bot-customization/)): "What runs it?" first (Apple on-device, each API connection, and coding agents, greyed on iPhone with "Coding agents run on a Mac."), then the character live in its dock ring on its palette with a coral dice button (a new character that keeps the owner's colors, expression, accessory, and dock settings), the name with its own dice, the engine and job under it, and closed drawers: Look (body, palette, body color, accent color, eyes, expression, on its head, wearing, holding, each option drawn on the character itself; rosy cheeks), Personality (starts as, its job, tone, and the owner's own words), Brain (runs on, model, effort), Context, Permissions, and Dock (size in the dock, the ring: engine color, my color, or none). On the Mac the form beside the dock adds what the bot chirps about. A new bot starts on the default model.
- **KemoSabe's settings** (tapping KemoSabe in Settings, Bots): its cloud in a ring of its color, its name, "Your secure assistant · Apple on-device", and its color as ten swatches (coral first), with "KemoSabe is always the same: its cloud, its name, and Apple’s on-device model on this iPhone. Its color is yours to pick." No dice, name field, character, engine, or permissions. Its color tints its card, its avatar's ring and lock, the searching dots, and the consent and share buttons. On the Mac, whether it chirps is in Settings, KemoSabe.

## The Mac app

The app in `apps/macos` (`com.zlichtman.tsukumo.mac`): the side dock of your bots, from the menu bar, on TsukumoDock and TsukumoUI.

- **It's the side dock, always.** Opening Tsukumo shows the dock (after the first run). No Dock icon: nothing chooses where the bots live.
- **The menu bar item** is Tsukumo's mark (a template image). Its menu, short and plain: Show Dock or Hide Dock, Open Together, Add a Bot…, a divider, Settings… (⌘,), a divider, Quit Tsukumo (⌘Q). Hide Dock puts the dock away until Show Dock, opening a chat, or the next launch.
- **The dock's right-click menu** is like the Dock's own: Position on Screen (edge, then top, center, bottom), Automatically Hide, Magnification, then Add a Bot… and Settings…. Everything else about the dock's look is in Settings, Dock, once.
- **Settings** is one window (780 × 580, at least 700 × 480) with a 200 pt sidebar like System Settings: each page an SF Symbol on a 22 pt colored tile (Account blue, Bots orange, Models purple, KemoSabe coral, Dock teal, General gray) and its name in 13 pt; the selected row in the accent with white text. The page's title (title 3, semibold) heads the right side over a hairline, then grouped `Form` sections. The pages follow the iPhone's Settings in order and names where they overlap, then the Mac's own:
  - **Account:** the account block (above), the footer on what signing in or out does, and "What syncs".
  - **Bots:** "Your bots", a row per bot (its 32 pt face, name, its job and engine, a chevron; KemoSabe shows its color as a dot), each opening its editor in a sheet (KemoSabe's: its color only); Make a bot; right-click to move or remove a bot.
  - **Models:** LLM and System One (segmented). LLM: On this Mac (Apple on-device's status), Default (New bots start on), API models (each with its mark, name, and "model · key on this Mac" or "No key yet"; click to edit, right-click to remove; Connect an API model: Claude, OpenAI, Other). The connection sheet: name, address (Other), the key (saved in this Mac's Keychain only), the model (the provider's list once the key is checked). System One: Untagged messages.
  - **KemoSabe:** Its color (its face and the ten swatches), What KemoSabe may read (Calendar, Reminders, each a switch and, when on, How private), Bots KemoSabe answers (Ask Again), Speaking up (chirps, and about what), Journal.
  - **Dock:** Look (Style, Size, Magnification and Magnified size, Spacing, Corners, Separator), Indicators (working or waiting on you, which engine runs each bot, names on hover), Position (Edge, Along the edge, Automatically hide, Hide after), Characters (Animation, Sleep at night, Chirp sounds).
  - **General:** Open at Login (on by default after the first run for a copy in /Applications; "Allow Tsukumo in System Settings, General, Login Items." in orange when macOS asks), and the version.

## The side dock

TsukumoDock: a Dock on its side, meant to look as if it belonged beside macOS's own. It holds KemoSabe first, then the owner's bots, then **+** and **Together**. Pictures: [`mac-preview/`](mac-preview/).

- **The shelf** (`DockShelfView`). Styles (`DockStyle`): **Glass** (default: Liquid Glass, `glassEffect(.regular)`, never a blur imitation), **Tinted glass** (the glass with the accent), **Solid**, and **Minimal** (no shelf, only the bots). The Dock's proportions: padding 14% of a tile, corners 40% of the shelf's thickness (15 to 50%); tiles start at the system Dock's size (`com.apple.dock` `tilesize`, else 48 pt; 32 to 80) with 10% between (4 to 30%); a 1 pt separator before **+** and **Together**, like the Dock's before the Trash (it can be off). Working indicator (`DockIndicator`): a 4 pt dot on the edge side beside bots that are working or need you (default), a ring around the tile, or none. Which engine runs each bot (`DockEngineMark`): a thin ring at its feet in the engine's color (default), its mark as a small badge, or nothing. A tile lifts to 105% under the pointer and dips to 93% when pressed. Names on hover in a small glass capsule, as plain text, or not at all.
- **Where it sits.** The right edge, centered, by default; the left edge, and the top, center, or bottom of either, in Settings. It's a floating panel on every Space and over full-screen apps that never activates Tsukumo. **Hiding** (on by default): a sliver at the edge (brighter while a bot needs you) until the pointer reaches it, then the shelf slides out (a fade with Reduce Motion) and back after the chosen delay (None, Short, Medium, Long) unless a bubble or speech bubble is out. **Magnification** (off unless the Dock's is on) grows tiles toward the pointer to the magnified size (40 to 128 pt).
- **Characters.** Each tile is a character: KemoSabe's companion artwork, or the bot's own clay character (TsukumoUI's `ClayPainter`: a big head on a small body, soft clay lighting, glossy eyes, its topper, accessory, and prop), at the bot's own size in the dock. Looping states play as Core Animation keyframes baked once (`ClaySprites`), so a resting dock doesn't redraw; one-off moments draw live on a 30 fps clock and stop. What a character acts out, most urgent first (`DockCharacterState.resolve`): **needs you** (a question it asked KemoSabe waits on the owner: a raised hand and an orange badge), **chirping** (a hop), **talking**, **working** (a coding bot at work types), **thinking** (until words come), **done** (a little celebration for 4 seconds after a reply), **asleep** (at night, 11 pm to 7 am, when Sleep at night is on, or while tucked away), and **idle** (breathing and blinking). KemoSabe is at its computer while it reads for a bot. Animation: Lively, Calm, or Still; Reduce Motion is always Still, and each state still reads by pose and badge.
- **Click** a character to open its chat beside the dock, again to close it. **Click-and-hold** pokes it (a wiggle); a **double-click** makes it giggle; **drag** it along the dock to reorder (KemoSabe stays first).
- **A bot's chat** is TsukumoUI's chat at Mac sizes (`ChatDensity.compact`, 13 pt body) in a bubble beside the tile, with the dock's own composer (`DockComposer`). The header: the bot's face, name, and state, a ⋯ menu (Edit <Name>…, or KemoSabe's Color…; Clear conversation; Remove from dock), and ×. KemoSabe's card, its consent prompt, and its share card work exactly as on iPhone, and a tile needs you while one waits.
- **Together:** one thread with every bot, chips above the composer to tag who gets a message ("@name" tags too); with nothing tagged it goes to the bot you last talked to.
- **Speech bubbles** (`DockCallout`): 250 pt wide, on the glass with a small tail toward the tile, for 6 seconds when a bot chirps in or finishes while you aren't looking; clicking one opens its chat.
- **Chirps** (`DockChirps`): a bot speaks up on its own, from fixed rules run on this Mac (no model and no service decides): a Calendar event or reminder coming up within its window (30 minutes to 1 day, optionally only items with certain words), from sources KemoSabe may read and only items the policy lets be read on this device; a bot finishing while you weren't looking; and a bot needing you. A chirp is shown on this Mac only and never goes to a bot. Chirp sounds are off by default.
- **Work from a coding bot** (`DockWorkCue`, filled by the app from its coding agents): the plan's step and a progress ring on the tile, the file it's in, a tests badge (running, passed, failed), and when it's ready for review; `EditorFollower` can open its folder in the owner's editor and follow its edits. The coding agents' real adapters aren't connected in the app yet.

## Type, color, and motion

- **Color.** The chat's palette is KemoSabe's Apricot (`TsukumoTheme`): plum `211B2C` and cream ink in dark, paper `FBF6EE` and plum ink in light, coral `EF705B` for the accent. Secondary text is the ink at about 60%, fills at 5 to 6.5%, hairlines at 8 to 10%. Warnings use orange; destructive actions use the system destructive role.
- **Type.** The system font, sized as the website demo's (`TsukumoType`): it scales with the owner's text size on iPhone; Mac UI text is 13 pt.
- **Motion.** Short ease-outs for presses, hovers, and disclosures. Every `TimelineView` in TsukumoDock and TsukumoUI uses `.periodic(from: .now, by: 1.0 / 30)`, since macOS stops a display-linked schedule in a panel it reports as hidden. Respect Reduce Motion everywhere.
- **Accessibility.** Every icon button has a label; state goes in the label or value (a tile reads its name and state); selection uses the `isSelected` trait. Accessibility identifiers stay stable across builds for UI tests.

## Checking a change

Look at both appearances (light and dark) and with VoiceOver on the changed controls. On the Mac, check Settings at its minimum size and the dock on each edge; on iPhone, check the smallest supported size with large text. Refresh the pictures in `design/` when a screen changes (DEVELOPMENT.md says how).
