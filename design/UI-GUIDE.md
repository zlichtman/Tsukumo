# UX/UI guide: iPhone

How Tsukumo's screens look and behave in the iPhone app (`apps/ios`), built on TsukumoKit's TsukumoUI. When code and this guide disagree, fix one of them in the same change. The standing rules are in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md); they win over this guide.

Wording (AGENTS.md rule 11): anything a person sees says KemoSabe, or the companion's own name, never "Kemo"; a person's agent is "<Name>'s secure assistant".

Pictures, light and dark: [`onboarding/`](onboarding/) (the first run, `iphone-1-welcome-*` to `iphone-3-connect-*`; the chat, `iphone-4-chat-*`; Your chats, `iphone-chats-*`; Account, signed in and out, `iphone-account-*`; and What KemoSabe may read, `iphone-kemosabe-sources-*`) and [`bot-customization/`](bot-customization/) (Settings, `iphone-bots-*`; the model sheet, `iphone-model-sheet-*`; KemoSabe's palettes, `iphone-kemosabe-palettes-*`; and a bot's settings, `iphone-service-bot-*`).

## Design philosophy

Tsukumo should feel like one calm object in your hand: the chat, and everything around it, in KemoSabe's colors, moving the same way, reachable with your thumb. A great panel next to a stock gray sheet is a bug, not a style. These hold everywhere, and every change is checked against them:

1. **One theme, everywhere.** Every screen, sheet, list, and picker sits on `TsukumoTheme`'s page and surfaces with its ink and accent. Nothing shows a bare system gray, an unthemed sheet, or a stock segmented control. If a screen needs something the design system doesn't have, add it there, then use it.
2. **Thumb first.** What you do often is at the bottom: the composer and its controls, and your chats. The top of the screen is for reading, not for buttons.
3. **Things come up, not in.** Secondary places are bottom sheets (Your chats, the model sheet, a bot's settings) that start at the size of what they show and pull up for more, never side panels that cover half the screen.
4. **System glass for navigation, the theme for content.** Toolbar buttons, close buttons, and search are the system's Liquid Glass; what they sit on is Tsukumo's.
5. **One control per job.** No duplicated buttons: one close per sheet, one way to each place. Settings opens from your picture at the top right, nowhere else.
6. **One catalog for Settings.** Settings follow one order and one set of names from TsukumoUI's `SettingsCatalog` (Account, Bots, Models, Connections), and the first run and the bots' settings are TsukumoUI's views.
7. **KemoSabe is always the same.** Its look, its name, Apple's on-device model, its job, and its scope never change; its companion palette and figure are what the owner picks. Every other bot is the owner's, made here (a name, a job, what they tell it, an engine of theirs, and a character), wearing one of Tsukumo's own characters, a Codex pet's still picture synced from a Mac, or its service's mark.
8. **Say what happened.** KemoSabe's card shows what was shared, what stayed on the device, and what was never read. A build without a capability explains itself in calm secondary text, never as a warning.
9. **Quiet chrome.** The accent marks state (selected, tagged, waiting on you), not decoration. Taps press in a little and give a light haptic; nothing else moves for show.
10. **A service is shown by its own mark,** plainly, the way an app says what it works with: never redrawn into a character.
11. **Not a social app.** No followers, friends, contacts lists, or feeds. You have a picture and a name, and that's the profile.

## Design system

TsukumoUI's `Style.swift` (colors, type) and `DesignSystem.swift` (everything else). Use these; don't restyle a screen by hand.

- **Colors** (`TsukumoTheme`): `background` (the chat's page: plum `211B2C` / paper `FBF6EE`), `sheet` (a sheet's page, a step lighter in dark), `surface` (cards and list rows, solid), `fill` (the ink at 5 to 6.5%, for the composer and bubbles), `hairline`, `ink`, `secondary`, `accent` (coral `EF705B`), `selected` (the accent at 16 to 24%, for a chosen segment or chip), `onAccent`.
- **Metrics** (`TsukumoMetrics`): 16 pt gutters, 22 pt card corners, 14 pt row corners, 38 pt controls, and one spring (0.32 s, a little bounce) for everything that moves.
- **Screens:** `.tsukumoScreen()` on every List or Form (`sheet: true` inside a sheet), `.tsukumoRows()` on its sections (the helper wraps a list's content in a `Group`), `.tsukumoSheet()` on every sheet (its page color and grabber), `.fittedSheet()` for a sheet as tall as its content that pulls up to full height.
- **Controls:** `PressableButtonStyle` (presses to 92%), `ControlCircle` (a 38 pt round icon button with a light haptic), `TsukumoSegmented` (the segmented picker: options in a capsule on `surface`, the chosen one on `selected`, sliding between them, with a selection haptic), `TsukumoEmptyState` (an icon on an accent-tinted circle, a title, one line), and the power slider (`PowerSlider`, `EffortHeat`, `SparkleField` in `PowerControls.swift`).

## The iPhone app

The app in `apps/ios`, on TsukumoUI (`TsukumoKit/Sources/TsukumoUI`). Pictures: `apps/ios/Screenshots/` (Settings, Models, Voice: `voice-settings-dark.png`).

- **One page: the chat.** Its header is Tsukumo's mark and wordmark, and your picture at the top right (38 pt; your photo, or your initials on coral), which opens Settings as a sheet. No tab bar.
- **Your chats** (`ConversationsSheet`; the composer's chats button): a bottom sheet at half height, pulled up for more, titled "Your chats" with the system close button. New chat and Activity (with its count) on top, then your chats newest first (the bots that spoke, the title, when it last moved; the open chat on `selected`; swipe to delete), and the search field ("Search chats", matching a chat's title or any message). Picking a chat closes the sheet and shows it. Nothing yet says "No chats yet".
- **A chat's menu** (touch and hold one in Your chats): Keep on This iPhone (a Device only chat: it leaves iCloud and your other devices, and shows a small iPhone mark) or Sync This Chat, and Delete.
- **The chat looks like the website demo.** Apricot: plum `211B2C` behind cream text in dark, warm paper `FBF6EE` in light, coral `EF705B` for KemoSabe's card and send. 24 pt gutters, 26 pt between rows. The owner's message: a bubble at the ink's 6.5%, 20 pt corners, 15 pt padding, at most 228 pt of text (so a long line wraps as on the website). A bot's reply: its 28 pt avatar, its name (caption, semibold, secondary), and the text (body, 4 pt line spacing). A bot is its service's mark on a rounded tile (`ServiceMarkView`); KemoSabe is its face in a circle (`KemoSabeFace`).
- **KemoSabe's card:** KemoSabe's avatar with a ring and lock in its color, its name, and a card (its color at 7%, 20% stroke, 16 pt corners, 12 pt padding, at most 460 pt): "Claude asked KemoSabe" (caption, semibold, `lock.shield`), the question in quotes, then "Why: …" and "Looking on this iPhone…" with the searching dots while it reads, or the consent prompt (Allow always, Allow once, Don't allow) the first time, or a share card for one Sensitive item (Share, Don't share); then the answer (body, semibold), "Stayed on this iPhone: …" (`lock`), "Not read: …", "Not shared: …", "Shared: “…”", and "On this iPhone · Apple on-device" (`apple.intelligence`). The bot's working row steps aside while its card is live. "Answered Claude: “…”" shows in a capsule at the top for 6 seconds.
- **The stage:** a new chat shows KemoSabe (200 pt; 160 pt with the demo row, so it all fits), "What’s on your mind?" (34 pt, semibold), "Make room for your day.", three suggestions (Plan my day, Think something through, Write a message, each filling in a draft), and **See how KemoSabe works** (on `selected` with a coral stroke and a play mark).
- **See how KemoSabe works** (`DemoScreen`, full screen): the website demo in its own chat, on sample chats, labelled "Demo · sample chats" beside a close button. It types "find a date spot for Sarah and I tonight", Claude asks KemoSabe, and the first-time consent card waits for you (Allow always, Allow once, Don't allow); then KemoSabe looks, answers "After 7 tonight", and Claude books the table. Nothing of yours is read, nothing is saved, and there's no microphone. Close goes back to your chat. Once you send, KemoSabe (150 pt) stays above the conversation, thinking while it reads, until the first reply or answer, then steps away.
- **The composer**, one card at the bottom (26 pt corners on `fill`): the chat's bots as chips when there's more than one (avatar and name; tap to tag, tagged ones on the accent; hold to talk to that bot), the message field ("Message Claude…", naming who it goes to), and one row in thumb reach: the **model chip** (the bot the message goes to: its mark and model, "Opus 5.5 · High" once a power is picked, a hare when it's fast; "On-device" with a lock for KemoSabe), then your chats (`bubble.left.and.bubble.right`), New chat (`square.and.pencil`), the microphone, and send (coral; stop while bots work), each 38 pt. "KemoSabe assists on this iPhone" under them. Typing "@" offers the bots as a strip above.
- **The model sheet** (`ModelSheet`, from the model chip; `.fittedSheet()`): the chat's bots as chips to switch between (when there's more than one), the bot (its avatar, name, and model), then **Power** (the effort as a big rounded word in the slider's heat color, "default" beside it when it's the model's own, a Default button otherwise, and the power slider: one stop per effort the model takes, the thumb on the model's default until you move it; "More power thinks longer and costs more."), **Speed** (Standard or Fast, only where the model offers fast mode: Claude Opus 5.5, 5, and 4.8 on Anthropic's API; "Answers up to 2.5× sooner, at twice the price."), and **Model** (its name; tap to open every engine's models, the current one checked, one without a key here greyed with why). Changes are the bot's own and are saved when the sheet closes. For KemoSabe, one line: it always runs on Apple's on-device model, on this iPhone.
- **Talking:** tap the microphone and talk; it sends when you stop talking (or tap it again to send sooner), like a typed message, so tags and "@name" work. With something already typed, what you say is added to the draft instead, for you to send. Hold the microphone and let go to send. Touch and hold a bot's chip to talk only to that bot; letting go sends. While it listens, the field becomes a strip: a five-bar meter in coral, your words as Apple's recognizer hears them ("Listening…" until then; "Writing it down…" while Whisper reads it), and × to cancel. A problem shows for a moment above the composer ("Didn’t catch that. Try again.", or where to allow the microphone). The reply is spoken in the bot's voice while it streams in, a sentence at a time, when replies are spoken (Settings, Models, Voice); talking again stops it.
- **Activity** (from Your chats or You): the segmented filter (All, KemoSabe, System One, Bots; `TsukumoSegmented`), days newest first, each row on `surface` with its icon, title, kind, time, and detail; "Nothing yet" when empty. Clear Activity is in its … menu. Tapping an item opens its chat.
- **Settings** (`SettingsScreen`, a sheet from your picture, with the system close button): your picture and name at the top (`ProfileHeader`): your photo, or your initials on coral, 64 pt, with a camera badge (tap to pick a photo, kept on this iPhone only; touch and hold to remove it), and your name ("You" until you sign in) with sync's state, or, in a build that can't sign in, "Your bots and chats stay on this iPhone". Then Bots, then Models, Connections, Activity, and Notifications (on or off, with iOS's own permission behind it), and the version. Search is at the bottom. Pages push inside the sheet; a bot's settings and a connection open as sheets.

## Type, color, and motion

- **Color.** The chat's palette is KemoSabe's Apricot (`TsukumoTheme`, Design system above): plum `211B2C` and cream ink in dark, paper `FBF6EE` and plum ink in light, coral `EF705B` for the accent. Secondary text is the ink at about 60%, fills at 5 to 6.5%, hairlines at 8 to 10%. Warnings use orange; destructive actions use the system destructive role.
- **Launch.** The launch screen is the chat's page (`LaunchBackground`: paper, or plum in dark), never black.
- **Type.** The system font, sized as the website demo's (`TsukumoType`): it scales with the owner's text size.
- **Motion.** `TsukumoMetrics.spring` for what moves (a segment, a disclosure, the model list), short ease-outs for presses. TsukumoUI's `TimelineView`s use a periodic schedule (`.periodic(from:by:)`: 1/30 s for KemoSabe and the chat, a sheet's own tick for a character). Respect Reduce Motion everywhere.
- **Accessibility.** Every icon button has a label; state goes in the label or value; selection uses the `isSelected` trait. Accessibility identifiers stay stable across builds for UI tests.

## Checking a change

Check it against the design philosophy above (one theme, thumb first, sheets that come up). Look at both appearances (light and dark) and with VoiceOver on the changed controls; check the smallest supported iPhone size with large text. Refresh the pictures in `design/` when a screen changes (DEVELOPMENT.md says how).
