# UX/UI guide: iPhone and Mac

How Tsukumo's screens look and behave, so the iPhone app (`apps/ios`) and the Mac app (`apps/macos`, Tsukumo's Dock) stay consistent. Both are built on TsukumoKit's TsukumoUI, and the Mac's side dock on TsukumoDock. When code and this guide disagree, fix one of them in the same change. The standing rules are in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md); they win over this guide.

Wording (AGENTS.md rule 14): anything a person sees says KemoSabe, or the companion's own name, never "Kemo"; a person's agent is "<Name>'s secure assistant".

Pictures, light and dark: [`onboarding/`](onboarding/) (the first run on both devices), [`bot-customization/`](bot-customization/) (KemoSabe's palettes, a bot's page, `mac-bot-panel-*`, a brought-in bot's, `mac-bot-panel-grok-*`, Add a Bot, `mac-add-bot-*`, and on iPhone Settings, Bots, `iphone-bots-*`, and a bot's settings, `iphone-service-bot-*`), [`mac-settings/`](mac-settings/) (every Mac Settings page, KemoSabe in each of its moods, `mac-kemosabe-states-*`, the dock with a lineup and its Settings tile, `mac-dock-lineup-*`, Muse's and Grok's pages in Settings, Bots, `mac-settings-bot-muse-*` and `mac-settings-bot-grok-*`, with the KemoSabe, Gateway, and Dock pages also at full length: `mac-settings-kemosabe-full-*`, `mac-settings-gateway-full-*`, `mac-settings-dock-full-*`, `mac-settings-dock-color-*`; the Gateway page with the public address on, `mac-settings-gateway-relay-*`; the gateway's card in KemoSabe's chat, `mac-gateway-card-*`, and its sign-in window, `mac-gateway-sign-in-*`, also as it looks through the relay, `mac-gateway-sign-in-relay-*`), and [`mac-preview/`](mac-preview/) (the side dock beside a chat, a service's panel, `service-panel-*`, a bot listening, and Tinted glass beside Solid over a bright and a dark wallpaper, `tinted-glass-*`, drawn with the glass's stand-in), and [`claude-bot/`](claude-bot/) (the Claude bot's panel: its tasks, a finished task, one waiting on you, and a new task).

## Principles

1. **One control per job.** No duplicated buttons: one Settings window on the Mac, one close button per sheet. On a Mac, Tsukumo is the side dock: nothing chooses where the bots live.
2. **The same app on both devices.** Settings follow one order and one set of names (Account, Bots, Models, KemoSabe), Bots lists every service in the same order, the first run and the bots' settings are the same TsukumoUI views, and a device adds only what's its own (the Mac's Gateway, Dock, and General pages, and each service's connect page on the Mac).
3. **KemoSabe is always the same.** Its look, its name, Apple's on-device model, its job, and its scope never change; its companion palette and figure are what the owner picks. Every other bot is the owner's: made here (a name, a job, what they tell it, an engine of theirs, and a character) or brought in (their ChatGPT dot, an agent signed in to KemoSabe, Tsukumo's Claude bot), wearing one of their Codex pets or its service's mark.
4. **Say what happened.** KemoSabe's card shows what was shared, what stayed on the device, and what was never read. A build without a capability explains itself in calm secondary text, never as a warning.
5. **Quiet chrome.** Icon buttons are borderless with a soft hover or pressed highlight. The accent marks state (selected, tagged, waiting on you), not decoration.
6. **A service is shown by its own mark,** plainly, the way an app says what it works with: never redrawn into a character.

## The iPhone app

The app in `apps/ios`, on TsukumoUI (`TsukumoKit/Sources/TsukumoUI`), which the Mac app shares. Pictures: `apps/ios/Screenshots/` (Settings, Models, Voice: `voice-settings-dark.png`).

- **The chat is the whole app.** No tab bar. The header: the drawer button (`sidebar.leading`) at the left, Tsukumo's mark and wordmark, and Settings (`gearshape`) at the right, both 42 pt round buttons at the ink's 6.5% fill. Settings and Activity open as sheets with Done.
- **The conversations drawer** slides in from the left (the button, or a swipe from the left 16 pt), up to 330 pt or 86% of the width, over the chat dimmed to 40% black (tap it, or swipe left, to close). From the top: "Chats", New chat (a coral-tinted row), your chats newest first (the bots that spoke, the title, when it last moved; the open chat highlighted; swipe to delete), and Activity at the bottom with its count. Picking a chat closes the drawer and shows it.
- **The drawer's chats** have a menu (touch and hold): Keep on This iPhone (a Device only chat: it leaves iCloud and your Mac, and shows a small iPhone mark) or Sync This Chat, and Delete.
- **The chat looks like the website demo.** Apricot: plum `211B2C` behind cream text in dark, warm paper `FBF6EE` in light, coral `EF705B` for KemoSabe's card and send. 24 pt gutters, 26 pt between rows. The owner's message: a bubble at the ink's 6.5%, 20 pt corners, 15 pt padding, at most 228 pt of text (so a long line wraps as on the website). A bot's reply: its 28 pt avatar, its name (caption, semibold, secondary), and the text (body, 4 pt line spacing). A bot is its service's mark on a rounded tile (`ServiceMarkView`); KemoSabe is its face in a circle (`KemoSabeFace`).
- **KemoSabe's card:** KemoSabe's avatar with a ring and lock in its color, its name, and a card (its color at 7%, 20% stroke, 16 pt corners, 12 pt padding, at most 460 pt): "Claude asked KemoSabe" (caption, semibold, `lock.shield`), the question in quotes, then "Why: …" and "Looking on this iPhone…" with the searching dots while it reads, or the consent prompt (Allow always, Allow once, Don't allow) the first time, or a share card for one Sensitive item (Share, Don't share); then the answer (body, semibold), "Stayed on this iPhone: …" (`lock`), "Not read: …", "Not shared: …", "Shared: “…”", and "On this iPhone · Apple on-device" (`apple.intelligence`). The bot's working row steps aside while its card is live. "Answered Claude: “…”" shows in a capsule at the top for 6 seconds.
- **The stage:** a new chat shows KemoSabe (200 pt), "What’s on your mind?" (34 pt, semibold), "Make room for your day.", and three suggestions (Plan my day, Think something through, Write a message). Once you send, KemoSabe (150 pt) stays above the conversation, thinking while it reads, until the first reply or answer, then steps away.
- **The composer:** the message field ("Message Claude…", naming who it goes to), then the bots as chips (avatar and name; tagged ones coral), the microphone (38 pt, the ink's fill; coral with a stop square while it listens), and send (coral circle; stop while bots work); "KemoSabe assists on this iPhone" under them. Typing "@" offers the bots as a strip above.
- **Talking** (the same as the Mac's, below): tap the microphone and talk; it sends when you stop talking (or tap it again to send sooner), like a typed message, so tags and "@name" work. With something already typed, what you say is added to the draft instead, for you to send. Hold the microphone and let go to send. Touch and hold a bot's chip to talk only to that bot; letting go sends. While it listens, the field becomes a strip: a five-bar meter in coral, your words as Apple's recognizer hears them ("Listening…" until then; "Writing it down…" while Whisper reads it), and × to cancel. A problem shows for a moment above the composer ("Didn’t catch that. Try again.", or where to allow the microphone). The reply is spoken in the bot's voice while it streams in, a sentence at a time, when replies are spoken (Settings, Models, Voice); talking again stops it.
- **Activity:** a segmented filter (All, KemoSabe, System One, Bots), days newest first, each row with its icon, title, kind, time, and detail.

## The Mac app

The app in `apps/macos` (`com.zlichtman.tsukumo.mac`): the side dock of your bots, from the menu bar, on TsukumoDock and TsukumoUI.

- **It's the side dock, always.** Opening Tsukumo shows the dock (after the first run). No Dock icon: nothing chooses where the bots live.
- **The menu bar item** is Tsukumo's mark (a template image). Its menu, short and plain: Show Dock or Hide Dock, Open Together, a divider, Settings… (⌘,), Check for Updates (no ellipsis, as in MacSpaces' menu: it opens Settings, General, and checks at once), a divider, Quit Tsukumo (⌘Q). Hide Dock puts the dock away until Show Dock, opening a chat, or the next launch.
- **The dock's right-click menu** is like the Dock's own: Position on Screen (edge, then top, center, bottom), Automatically Hide, Magnification, then Settings…. Everything else about the dock's look is in Settings, Dock, once.
- **Settings** is one window (880 × 640, at least 720 × 520) in MacSpaces' design language, on Tsukumo's palette (TsukumoDock's `SettingsPage`, `SettingsCard`, `SettingsRow`; pictures in [`mac-settings/`](mac-settings/)):
  - **The sidebar** (214 pt, on the card color, a 1 pt hairline at its edge; below 720 pt wide it shrinks to 60 pt of icons): Tsukumo's icon (34 pt, rounded like a Dock icon, a soft shadow) beside "Tsukumo" (15 pt, bold), then Search as MacSpaces has it (a 34 pt capsule, "Search settings", the ink at 6%, 9% and a hairline while focused, `magnifyingglass` and a clear button; ⌘F focuses it; in the 60 pt sidebar a magnifier opens it in a popover). While you type, the page becomes Search ("N matching settings"): one card per result (its page's symbol in coral, its name, where it is, a chevron), ↑ and ↓ move the highlight, Return opens it, Esc clears; opening one clears the search and scrolls its page to the card, outlined in coral (`mac-settings-search-*`). Then one 34 pt row per page: a plain monochrome SF Symbol (13 pt, semibold, the ink at 65%) and the page's name (13 pt, medium). The page you're on is a soft gray fill (the ink at 6.5% in light, 9% in dark; 9 pt corners) with a 2 × 16 pt coral bar on its leading edge, its symbol in coral and its name semibold; the row under the pointer gets a fainter fill.
  - **The page:** the paper or plum page, 28 pt padding, a title (26 pt, bold) and one line under it (13 pt, secondary), then cards 18 pt apart. Each card: a hairline border (the ink at 10 to 12%), 14 pt corners, 16 pt padding, a fill a little lighter than the page, and a header of an SF Symbol and its title (13 pt, semibold, secondary). List rows: a 30 pt icon tile (or the bot's face, or the engine's mark), a title (13 pt, medium) with an optional count (tertiary), a line under it (caption, secondary), and a chevron (11 pt, tertiary) where it opens something, divided by hairlines. Toggles are checkboxes; pickers are menus or, for a few short choices, segmented. Tsukumo has no appearance choice of its own (it follows the Mac's), so there's no System, Light, Dark picker.
  - The pages follow the iPhone's Settings in order and names where they overlap, then the Mac's own, each with its line:
  - **Account** ("Who you are, and what goes where."): Sign in (the hero above) or Your account (the name, the pill, Sign Out), then What goes where.
  - **Bots** ("KemoSabe, your bots, Muse, and the agents that ask KemoSabe."; `BotsSettings.swift`; October 8, the owner: "all the bots go into the bots section"): if 2.05 moved the custom bots of an older build, its card first, with Show in Finder and OK. Then **Bots** (October 8, the owner: made here and connected are two kinds): KemoSabe (its face, its subtitle, its palette's name) and each bot that runs on the owner's engines on this Mac (its character, its name, where it's from and what runs it, "Needs you" in orange while it waits), each with a chevron to its page, then **Add a Bot…**. Then **Connected**: each bot that lives elsewhere (Muse, gateway agents), then each service not connected yet (Muse, ChatGPT and dots, Claude.ai, Grok, OpenClaw: its mark, its status, "Connect", and a chevron to its page: a 620 × 680 sheet with how to connect it), then any agent to confirm ("This is…"). Then **Agents** (each agent signed in to the gateway; **Add an Agent with a Token…**). Headings are one or two plain words (Bots, Agents, Character, Palette, Voice, Details, Engine, Activity), never "Its …". Connect, per service:
    - **Claude:** Claude Code on this Mac (Installed, its version, the models it offers; or how to install it), the Claude API key (Add Key… or Change…, the editor from Models), Let Claude ask KemoSabe (the gateway's public address with Copy, and Claude's Connectors steps), and a card for Tsukumo's own Claude bot, whose panel takes the tile's place when it's ready (`ServiceBotPanelProvider`).

It holds KemoSabe first, then the owner's bots in their order, then **Add a Bot** (+, a tile like Together's that opens Add a Bot beside the dock), **Together**, and **Settings** always last. ## Type, color, and motion

- **Color.** The chat's palette is KemoSabe's Apricot (`TsukumoTheme`): plum `211B2C` and cream ink in dark, paper `FBF6EE` and plum ink in light, coral `EF705B` for the accent. Secondary text is the ink at about 60%, fills at 5 to 6.5%, hairlines at 8 to 10%. Warnings use orange; destructive actions use the system destructive role.
- **Type.** The system font, sized as the website demo's (`TsukumoType`): it scales with the owner's text size on iPhone; Mac UI text is 13 pt.
- **Motion.** Short ease-outs for presses, hovers, and disclosures. Every `TimelineView` in TsukumoDock and TsukumoUI uses `.periodic(from: .now, by: 1.0 / 30)`, since macOS stops a display-linked schedule in a panel it reports as hidden. Respect Reduce Motion everywhere.
- **Accessibility.** Every icon button has a label; state goes in the label or value (a tile reads its name and state); selection uses the `isSelected` trait. Accessibility identifiers stay stable across builds for UI tests.
