# Tsukumo's Dock 2.10 (build 210)

October 8, 2026. Bots made here and connected bots, sorted (the owner: "rethink how this works", "you can't seem to message the bot"). Bots are two kinds everywhere: made here, on your engines on this Mac (KemoSabe, Tsukumo's Claude bot, a Leafy of your own on Codex, a continued conversation, one you make), each with one Remove; and connected, living elsewhere (Muse, gateway agents, your ChatGPT dot), each with one Disconnect, which signs it out and takes it off the dock. Settings, Bots has Bots and Connected (with each service not connected yet, and agents to confirm); Add a Bot has Connect, Ready Made, Conversations, and New Bot. A bot's page runs Details, Engine or Connection, Access, Allowed, Voice, Chirps, and Activity, with Chat in its top bar; the chat has Settings. Your paired Muse chats from the dock: each chat is a side chat of your Muse, only your words go, and its answer shows here when Muse's response carries it. In Together, a bot addressed by name ("Leafy, …", "ask Muse …") gets the message without an "@". A tile's hover label says what a click does.

- **Where:** `https://zlichtman.com/downloads/Tsukumo-2.10.dmg`, with `/downloads/Tsukumo.dmg` redirecting to it, and the update feed `https://zlichtman.com/downloads/tsukumo.json` (version 2.10, build 210). Homebrew: `brew install --cask zlichtman/tap/tsukumo`.
- **DMG:** 26,619,924 bytes, SHA-256 `9e60a61edca1b16d9b34713acf56d7a0beb4bec1a53780304ce83e664fce573b`, notarized (submissions f5dae354-5bb8-4497-aa09-45a257325979 and d2c56d6e-84a4-443c-95be-d66f7d5ece6c), stapled, accepted by Gatekeeper as Notarized Developer ID.

The tap's push was refused because MacSpaces 2.90 had just been pushed to the same tap; the Tsukumo commit was rebased onto it, recommitted as the no-reply identity, and pushed by hand, then the download, redirect, feed, and `brew fetch` were checked against the SHA-256 and the build number committed.
