# Tsukumo's Dock 2.05 (build 205)

October 7, 2026. A bot for each service: the dock's lineup is KemoSabe, then each service you've connected (Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Muse, OpenClaw, Codex, Cursor Agent, Gemini CLI), with no bots of your own making; older custom bots move into their service's bot, with a backup first. Tsukumo's Claude bot lives on the dock's Claude tile: its tasks run in the background on your own Claude, ask KemoSabe through the gateway, and show on the tile (needs you, at work, done or didn't finish). Settings, Bots lists every service, and Settings has search, from the one catalog the iPhone shares. KemoSabe has its two-tone look. Sync is safer: chats merged on another device join here without losing messages or bringing a deleted chat back, a Device only chat is never joined, a chat's members the iPhone can't run stay in it, and sync only ever raises a chat's privacy. iCloud and Sign in with Apple stay off in this build (`TSUKUMO_CAPABILITIES=Local`).
