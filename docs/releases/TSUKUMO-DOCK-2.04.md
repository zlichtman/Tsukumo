# Tsukumo's Dock 2.04 (build 204)

October 7, 2026. KemoSabe answers other agents through the gateway: Settings, Gateway (off by default) serves eight typed tools over MCP on this Mac (free/busy, a contact, a question, listing and sharing files, message excerpts, and photos, and an Inbox for what agents deliver), with in-app OAuth sign-in, consent cards in the dock, standing permissions, per-agent and overall daily budgets, a disclosure ledger, and Revoke. Muse pairs as a gadget from the owner's phone (Settings, KemoSabe, Muse) and calls only through the gateway. Settings, Gateway, Public address connects to a Tsukumo relay for cloud agents (the relay isn't deployed yet). And Tsukumo now updates itself: Settings, General, Software Update, and Check for Updates in the menu bar menu, reading `tsukumo.json` from the owner's website. iCloud and Sign in with Apple stay off in this build (`TSUKUMO_CAPABILITIES=Local`).

- **Where:** the owner's website only: `https://zlichtman.com/downloads/Tsukumo-2.04.dmg`, with `/downloads/Tsukumo.dmg` and the old 2.00 to 2.03 links redirecting to it, and the update feed at `https://zlichtman.com/downloads/tsukumo.json` (version 2.04, build 204, the DMG's URL and SHA-256, minimum macOS 26.0). 2.03 was taken down. Homebrew wasn't updated; the `tsukumo` cask stays disabled.
- **DMG:** 24,009,750 bytes, SHA-256 `e819c6d1b7e5101183bc25bc415ab099cf75d7779624a19496bab072a8f100a2`, notarized (app: 08cffa87-a354-4c0a-b5ff-813af9250b4b, DMG: 0a90c83d-e82f-45ae-a4e7-125c9681d103), stapled, accepted by Gatekeeper as Notarized Developer ID.
- **How:** `scripts/release-mac.sh 204 --dry-run` built, tested (670 TsukumoKit tests), notarized, and checked it; the DMG and `tsukumo.json` were placed on the site by hand. An earlier 2.04 candidate built from `be019ad` (without Software Update) was notarized but never published.
- **Not checked live:** a real self-update (the first one is from 2.04 to a later build), a cloud agent through the relay, pairing with a real Muse app. DEVELOPMENT.md, known issues 10 to 13.
