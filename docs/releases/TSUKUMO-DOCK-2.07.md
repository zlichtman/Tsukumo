# Tsukumo's Dock 2.07 (build 207)

October 8, 2026. Your Leafy, working on the dock: Add a Bot offers a Leafy of your own on Codex (your ChatGPT dot's name and pet, a bot you chat with right on the dock, on your Codex; it doesn't share the dot's memory in ChatGPT), and the dot's own page has a message box that opens its conversation in Codex with your message typed in (OpenAI offers no way for another app to message a dot). Add a Bot also offers your recent Codex and Claude Code conversations on this Mac, each brought in as a bot whose chat continues that same session. Codex pets are offered only on bots that run on Codex; every other bot wears its service's mark (Tsukumo's own code-drawn characters were tried and left out: characters ship only when they're as good as Codex's pets and KemoSabe). You name each bot and say what it does. iCloud and Sign in with Apple stay off (`TSUKUMO_CAPABILITIES=Local`).
