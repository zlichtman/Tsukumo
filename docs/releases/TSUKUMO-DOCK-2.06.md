# Tsukumo's Dock 2.06 (build 206)

October 8, 2026. Your bots: the dock is KemoSabe plus the owner's own bots again, with the services as the engines underneath. A bot is brought in (the owner's ChatGPT dot, read from Codex on the Mac and opened in Codex; Tsukumo's Claude bot; an agent signed in to the KemoSabe gateway once the owner confirms its service) or made (a name, a job, what the owner tells it, an engine, and a character). A character is one of the owner's Codex pets, read from their own Codex and acting out the bot's state, or a pet they hatch in Codex; otherwise its service's mark. Add a Bot is the + tile after the bots; Settings, Bots lists your bots, then the services. A 2.05 dock moves once: the service bots the owner used stay as their own, with their chats, and confirmed callers come back as brought-in bots. Settings search works as MacSpaces' does. KemoSabe is its cloud again, with the Two-tone figure, redrawn as a small round 3D toy, as the owner's pick. iCloud and Sign in with Apple stay off in this build (`TSUKUMO_CAPABILITIES=Local`).
